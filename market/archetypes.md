# Archetypes → CV order (based on laptop-1 evidence + market pass 1, 2026-10-27)

Each archetype gets a **Senior** and a **Mid** variant. The difference:
- **Senior:** headline "Senior Frontend Engineer"; lead with ownership — built from scratch, designed the data layer, wrote the team guide, mentored 2, handled client feedback rounds.
- **Mid:** headline "Frontend Engineer"; lead with delivery and quality — features, tests, stack depth; fewer "led/designed" words. For big companies asking 5+ years.
